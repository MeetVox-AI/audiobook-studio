## What's new in 0.3.4

- **The voice engine updates itself.** When a new version of the app needs a newer engine, the app updates it for you: no Terminal command any more. With automatic updates on, it happens by itself at launch (or as soon as a conversion in progress finishes), with a progress line at the top of the window; with automatic updates off, the banner at the top offers **Update**. Only the engine code and its libraries are downloaded; your voices, models and audiobooks stay. Until it is updated, voice clean-up and **Change Recording…** are greyed out instead of failing with "No such option: --isolate".
- **Quiet app updates.** Updates no longer open a window. With automatic updates on (the default), a new version downloads in the background and installs when you quit; meanwhile an **Update Now** button next to **Settings** restarts the app into it. With automatic updates off, the same button offers **Update…**, and if you ignore it you keep your current version.
- The engine installer follows the project's new address, so copies installed before the move keep updating.

Updating from 0.3.3 may still show the update window one last time; from 0.3.4 on, updates are quiet. If you installed the engine yourself in Terminal before, there is nothing to do: the app updates it.
