## What's new in 0.3.6

- **Many more voices to choose from.** The voice library grows from 31 to 251 reference voices. French goes from 5 to 36 narrators; Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch, German and Italian have about 30 each, Chinese 14, and Arabic, Korean and Hindi a handful. Add a language in **Settings → Languages** to get its voices (about 10–17 MB for the larger ones).
- **Favourites.** Tap the star on any voice, then switch on **Favourites** in the Voices filters to see only the voices you like. Starred voices also come first in their list, and your stars are kept between launches.
- **Clips now match their words.** Voices cut from a longer recording are now cut at exactly the right place, so the voice you preview is the one the transcript describes. Downloads fetch only what they need (about 1 MB per voice) and retry when the server is busy.

New voices have not all been listened to yet, and a voice's gender is estimated from its pitch: star the ones you like, and tell us about any that sound wrong. Voices you already installed are unchanged; new ones appear when you add the language again.
