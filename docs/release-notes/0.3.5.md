## What's new in 0.3.5

- **New audiobook starts empty.** Once you start a conversion, the book moves into **Jobs** (finished or not), and **New audiobook** shows the drop zone for your next EPUB or PDF instead of reopening the book you just converted. A book you opened but have not started converting is kept while you look around. **Convert again with other settings** on a job still reopens that book.
